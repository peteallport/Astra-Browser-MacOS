# AstraBrowse icon proposals

Created September 8, 2026 with the built-in image generation tool. These are concept renders for review, not an installed app icon.

## Source and design interpretation

Reviewed the [GPT-6 Astra announcement](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/) live, including its interactive star-field hero. The observed visual language is near-black space, luminous white stars, occasional cool-blue and warm points, restrained white typography, and deep spatial movement. The icon materials and shapes below are original design interpretations of that visual language.

AstraBrowse's [product plan](../../planning.md) describes a macOS browser that presents websites through native interfaces. This suggests combining astronomical identity with a window or a transformation between interface layers.

## Proposals

| Concept | Strength | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Orbit A — recommended** | Strong distinctive silhouette; ties the Astra name to navigation; easiest to recognize in the Dock. | The browser function is implicit. |
| **Stellar Window** | Most direct connection between Astra's stars and a browser window; restrained and understandable. | More generic as a brand mark; thin dividers need simplification for tiny uses. |
| **Native Prism** | Best representation of a website becoming an organized native interface. | Most visual detail; less immediate at small sizes. |

[Open the comparison gallery](index.html) for equal-sized previews, light/dark backgrounds, and 16/32/64-pixel previews.

The PNGs retain the original generated images. A selected concept would still need production cleanup and separate small-size treatment. No application code or icon assets were replaced.

## Stellar Window

File: `01-stellar-window.png`

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original macOS app icon concept for AstraBrowse, a browser that turns existing websites into native interfaces. Generate ONE premium app icon, not a presentation sheet. A perfectly centered rounded-square app tile on a transparent exterior, straight-on orthographic view, no tilt of the tile itself. Tile occupies 88% of a square 1024x1024 canvas with even transparent margins. Near-black midnight blue tile (#080E14), a very subtle soft upper-left surface highlight and carefully controlled dimensionality. Restrained cool white and icy-blue accent light, a tiny hint of warm starlight is allowed. Inspired by a dark astronomical star-field announcement page with luminous white points and quiet scientific elegance. Give the foreground symbol a bold readable silhouette, 64% tile width, optically centered, visible at small Dock sizes. Restraint, precision, beautifully resolved materials, no noisy nebula, no purple gradient, no generic AI sparkle cluster, no OpenAI blossom, no lettering, no title, no text, no watermark, no device mockup.
Primary request: STELLAR WINDOW. A single elegant luminous browser-window frame as the primary emblem. Rounded horizontal rectangle with a subtly thick ice-white glass rim, a single horizontal toolbar separator near the top, no traffic light dots, and a short vertical sidebar separator on the left. The window is almost front-facing, with very subtle slab depth. In its main content space is one distinct luminous stellar point with short optical diffraction rays, slightly right of center; just three very small dim secondary stars. The inside of the window is deep midnight black, so the clear bright frame and a concentrated single light source carry the entire icon. The frame suggests looking into a new native web. Keep the frame thick and simple, the star small enough that it does not resemble a large generic AI four-point logo. Soft subtle bloom only, clean outside edges.
